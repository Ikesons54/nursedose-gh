    Quick reference

    The formulas

    Liquids and injections
    Ordered ÷ Stock × Volume
    Tablets
    Ordered ÷ Stock

    Unit conversions

    1 g= 1000 mg
    1 mg= 1000 mcg

    Always make the units match before you divide.

    A worked example

    Ordered: 500 mg. Stock: 1000 mg in 2 ml. How much do you give?

    Formula
    Ordered ÷ Stock × Volume
    Put in the numbers
    500 ÷ 1000 × 2
    Answer
    1 ml

    Before you give

    • Ask: does the answer make sense next to one full tablet or the full stock volume?
    • Tablets are usually given whole or as a half.
    • Check every dose with your tutor and your hospital’s protocol.
